About Karpasa Natural

Karpasa Natural Industry is a genuine Nepali luxury textile manufacturer specializing in premium cotton, high-performance fabrics for fashion, silk, banana fabric, alloy, cashmere, and fashion healthcare. We blend innovation with ethical practices to redefine modern textiles. Our mission is to provide high-performance, planet-friendly textiles that empower fashion brands to reduce their environmental footprint.
