About Us

Karpasa Natural Industry is a genuine Nepali luxury textile manufacturer specializing in premium cotton, high-performance fabrics for fashion, silk, banana fabric, alloy, and cashmere. We blend innovation with ethical practices to redefine modern textiles. Our mission is to provide high-performance, planet-friendly textiles that empower fashion brands to reduce their environmental footprint.

Our Mission

To promote innovation in Nepal’s plant-based and animal-based natural fiber sector, using organic and non-organic fibers for fabrics and ready-made garments, while supporting cultural heritage, economic growth, environmental sustainability, and improving livelihoods in poor communities.

Our Commitment

We are committed to using only natural fibers in our production, including the finest wool, banana fiber, silk, and alloy. All farming is conducted ethically, ensuring no animals are harmed during the process. We take pride that all the yarns used are Oeko-Tex certified and meet the highest standards.
