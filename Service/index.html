Chyangra Pashmina Textile

The fine Pashmina wool comes from the Himalayan Chyangra (Capra Hircus) goats of Nepal. Nepalese artisans have woven this luxurious fiber for over a thousand years.

Banana Fiber Textile

Banana fiber is a highly durable, biodegradable and eco-friendly natural fiber sourced from banana plant stems. Traditionally used across Asia, it has evolved into a popular material for sustainable luxury.
