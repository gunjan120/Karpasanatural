Chyangra Pashmina Textile

The fine Pashmina wool comes from the Himalayan Chyangra (Capra Hircus) goats of Nepal. Nepalese artisans have woven this luxurious fiber for over a thousand years. Known globally for its unmatched softness, warmth, and durability — Pashmina shawls and garments are handwoven on traditional looms. Today, the silk–pashmina blend is admired worldwide for its elegance and comfort.

Banana Fiber Textile

Banana fiber is a highly durable, biodegradable and eco–friendly natural fiber sourced from banana plant stems. Traditionally used across Asia, it has evolved into a popular material for ready–made garments, home decor, and handicrafts. Its natural sheen, lightweight feel, and sustainability make it a perfect alternative to synthetic fibers.
