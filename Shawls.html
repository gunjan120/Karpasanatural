Shawls

The Pasima Shawls by Karpasa Natural Industry are a distinct line of textiles that ingeniously merge luxury tradition with modern sustainable innovation. While the classic "Pashmina" shawl is historically made from the ultra-fine undercoat of the Himalayan Cashmere goat, Karpasa sets its product apart by incorporating natural banana fiber (bana fiber). This creative use of a renewable agricultural byproduct results in a lightweight, biodegradable shawl that retains a luxurious texture and appealing natural sheen. By blending this eco-friendly fiber, Karpasa offers a versatile, stylish, and durable accessory that honors the softness and elegance of a premium wrap while promoting a commitment to sustainable textile practices.
