What We Do

Handcrafted Traditional Artistry from Karpasa Natural Industry Pvt. Ltd.

1. Chyangra Pashmina Textile Readymade Products

The fine wool known as Pashmina comes from the undercoat of the Himalayan Mountain goat (Chyangra). Nepalese artisans have been weaving this luxurious fiber into shawls and blankets for over a thousand years. It is prized for its softness, warmth, and durability. We produce ready-made garments, shawls, blankets, and customized designs as per demand.

2. Himalayan Nettle or “Allo” Textile Readymade Products

Himalayan nettle (Allo) is a strong, smooth, lightweight, and durable natural fiber. We produce shawls, blankets, bags, yarn, and garments blended with cotton, silk, cashmere, and pashmina.

3. Banana Textile Readymade Products

Banana fiber is biodegradable, eco-friendly, lightweight, and durable. It is used for clothes, handbags, textile crafts, and home décor. We create sustainable natural fiber solutions using banana fiber.
