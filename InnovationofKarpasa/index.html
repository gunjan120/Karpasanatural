Himalayan Nettle Fiber (Allo)

Himalayan nettle fiber, or Allo is a sustainable fiber product central to Nepal's rural economy. Its commercial harvesting and processing into durable fiber provide critical cash income, supporting local livelihoods. This value chain, often managed through community-based enterprises, empowers women and fosters collective action. By transforming a wild resource into marketable textiles and crafts, it drives grassroots economic development, reduces migration, and strengthens community resilience, making it a vital tool for integrated rural development in Nepal.
