About Us

Karpasa Natural Industry is a genuine Nepali luxury textile manufacturer specializing in premium cotton, silk, banana fabric, alloy, and cashmere.

We blend innovation with ethical practices to redefine modern textiles. Our mission is to provide high-performance, planet-friendly textiles that empower fashion brands to reduce their environmental footprint.

Our Mission

To promote innovation in Nepal’s natural fiber sector, supporting cultural heritage and environmental sustainability while improving livelihoods in communities.

Our Commitment

We use only natural fibers and ethical farming. All yarns are Oeko-Tex certified, ensuring no animals are harmed and no chemicals compromise quality.
